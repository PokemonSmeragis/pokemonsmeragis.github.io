Obtainable Items

The useful items you can get in the demo (up to Route 120): Rare Candies, Heart Scales, Ability Capsules & Patches, PP Ups, Mints, TMs/HMs, evolution items, Mega Stones, held battle items and valuables to sell (with how much money they give). Shops are listed only for these. Each item is in the first split where you can grab it, following the Trainer Sheet; field moves count from the badge that unlocks them.
